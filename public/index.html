          How your files stay private
          
                
            
              • 
              Files are read in 64 KB pieces and encrypted with AES-256-GCM in your browser, away
              from the page's main thread.
            
                • 
            
              • 
              The key is made on the sender's device and lives only in the link after the #.
              Browsers never send that part to a server.
            
                • 
            
              • 
              The server only introduces the two browsers. After that they talk directly, and the
              room is deleted as soon as they connect.
            
                • 
            
              • 
              Nothing is written to a server. If a direct connection is impossible, an optional
              relay forwards data that is already encrypted.
            
                • 
          
              
        
              
      
              

      
              
        No accounts. No tracking. No third-party requests.
